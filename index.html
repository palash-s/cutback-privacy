Who we are

CutBack is an expense tracker that records your payments from the alerts your bank and payment apps already send you. It is made by the developer named on its Google Play listing (“we”); you can reach us at developer@edumatrix-ams.org.

What is stored, and where

Everything is stored only in the app’s private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read:

  • Your payments: amount, date, bank or app, account or card last digits, payee name or UPI ID, reference number, balance, and the text of the bank message it came from.
  • Bills read from messages: biller, amount, due date, last digits of the card or account, and the payment link from the message. Tapping “Pay now” opens that link in your browser; the app itself never goes online.
  • Pop’s comments (teasing or praise about your spending) are worked out on your phone from your own totals. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • What you add: categories, notes, splits, money owed to you, monthly plans and your name as your bank prints it (used only to recognise transfers between your own accounts).
  • A short on-device activity log (for example “SMS from a bank: payment saved”) so you can see why a banner did or did not appear. It never contains message text.

Android’s automatic cloud backup is switched off for this app, so your data is not copied to Google’s servers.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone, and the app has no way to do so. The only time data leaves the app is when you export a backup file: you choose where it is saved (for example your phone’s storage or Google Drive). That file contains your payments, including bank message text, so keep it private. Once saved somewhere else, it is covered by that service’s own terms.

Keeping or deleting your data

  • Delete a single payment from its detail screen.
  • Delete everything from Settings → Backup & data → Delete all data.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone.

Data is kept until you delete it. Because we never hold a copy, we cannot recover it for you; use the backup option if you want a copy.

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may access, correct and erase your personal data. In CutBack you can do all three directly in the app, because the data never leaves your phone. For any privacy question or grievance, contact us at the address below.

Children

CutBack is meant for adults managing their own bank accounts. It is not directed at children under 18.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released, and explain the change inside the app.
